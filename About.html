About Essie Cosmetics

Our Story

Essie Cosmetics was founded with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, natural beauty products that enhance your natural beauty without compromising your health or the environment.

Our journey began when our founder, Esther, noticed a gap in the market for affordable, natural beauty products that actually deliver results. After years of research and development, Essie Cosmetics was born.

Our Philosophy

We believe that beauty should be simple, accessible, and natural. All our products are carefully formulated with ethically sourced ingredients that are gentle on your skin and the planet.

Our Commitment

  • 100% natural ingredients
  • Cruelty-free testing
  • Environmentally conscious packaging
  • Affordable pricing
  • Community education and empowerment
